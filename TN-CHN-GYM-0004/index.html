Questions

Good to know

Everything you might want to check before your first session — or while comparing memberships.

Is there a joining fee or any hidden charge?
No joining fee on quarterly and annual memberships, and no hidden charges. You pay for the plan you choose, and for personal training only if you ask for it.
What equipment do you have?
Free weights up to 40 kg, racks, benches, cable stations, plate-loaded machines, treadmills, cycles, cross trainers and a functional area with ropes, kettlebells and boxes.
Can I pause or transfer my membership?
Yes. Monthly plans can be paused for medical reasons with a doctor's note, and annual memberships can be transferred once to a family member.
Do I need prior gym experience to join?
Not at all. Most of our members start as complete beginners. Your first session includes a free form check and a simple plan you can follow right away.
Are there separate timings for women?
Yes. We run dedicated ladies' batches with a female trainer available. Call +91 80983 33888 for the current batch schedule.
