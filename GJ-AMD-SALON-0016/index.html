Questions

Things clients ask us

Everything you might want to check before booking an appointment.

What are your opening hours?
We open at 9:30 AM and take the last appointment at 10:30 PM, all seven days. Evenings and weekends fill up first, so booking ahead helps.
How do you charge for hair colour?
Rates depend on hair length and the product used. We tell you the final price after a quick consultation and a patch test, before starting the colour.
Which payment modes do you accept?
Cash, all cards and UPI. Packages can be paid in two instalments for bridal bookings.
Which products do you use?
Professional brands for colour, keratin and treatments, all patch tested before use. Tell us about any allergies at the consultation.
Are the tools and towels clean?
Every comb, scissor and towel is sanitised after each client, and waxing uses disposable strips. Blades and files are single-use.
