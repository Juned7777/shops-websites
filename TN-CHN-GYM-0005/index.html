Questions

Before you join

Everything you might want to check before your first session — or while comparing memberships.

Do I need prior gym experience to join?
Not at all. Most of our members start as complete beginners. Your first session includes a free form check and a simple plan you can follow right away.
What equipment do you have?
Free weights up to 40 kg, racks, benches, cable stations, plate-loaded machines, treadmills, cycles, cross trainers and a functional area with ropes, kettlebells and boxes.
Is parking available?
Two-wheeler parking is available at the door and there is street parking for cars outside. Evenings after 7 PM are the busiest, so allow a few extra minutes.
Do you provide personal training and diet plans?
Both. Personal training is booked in packs of twelve sessions, and diet consultations give you a written plan built around home food.
What are your opening hours?
We open at 10:00 AM and close at 11:00 PM, all seven days. Early morning and late evening batches are busiest — afternoon is the quietest time to train.
