About us

Started with a grinder. Grew into Fort Kochi's meeting point.

It began as a tiny counter in Fort Kochi serving the neighbourhood one careful cup at a time. Word travelled the old-fashioned way — someone brought a friend.

The espresso is pulled at 9 bar, the cakes are cut thick, and the Wi-Fi is fast enough to work from. That balance is the whole point.

How we work

What makes a cup here different.

No syrups from a bottle, no pre-ground coffee, no reheated food. Just the small decisions that add up.

  1. 01

    Roasted in small batches

    Green beans are roasted in 5 kg lots and cupped before they ever reach the bar. If a batch isn't right, it doesn't go out.

  2. 02

    Ground the moment you order

    Beans are weighed, ground and extracted to order. The crema you see is minutes old — not hours.

  3. 03

    A kitchen, not a microwave

    Sauces, syrups, pesto and dressings are made each morning in our own kitchen. Nothing arrives frozen.

  4. 04

    Built for the neighbourhood

    Regulars get their usual, first-timers get honest recommendations, and nobody is rushed off a table.

Questions

Good to know

Everything you might want to check before you walk in — or while you're deciding what to order.

What are your opening hours?
We open at 8:00 AM and serve the full menu until 11:00 PM, all seven days. The last order is taken thirty minutes before closing.
Do you take table reservations?
Tables for two to four are first-come, first-served. For groups of six or more — birthdays, meet-ups or office lunches — call us on +91 484 221 5769 and we'll keep a table ready.
Do you do bulk or office orders?
We do — breakfast platters, sandwich trays and flask coffee for offices and events. Give us a day's notice on +91 484 221 5769 and we'll handle the rest.
Do you have vegetarian, vegan or Jain options?
Plenty. Around half the menu is vegetarian and several dishes can be made vegan on request. Tell us your preference and the kitchen will suggest what to order.
Can I buy your beans to take home?
Yes — 250 g and 500 g bags of our house blend and rotating single origins, ground to suit your brewer.
