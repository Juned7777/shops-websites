Our story

A bakery that starts at 4 AM.

Thea Patisserie! was never meant to be a factory bakery. It was meant to be the shop where the bread is still warm and the cake tastes like it was made at home.

Every recipe is tested in our own kitchen before it reaches the counter. If a bake is not good enough for our own family, it does not go on display.

Questions

Before you order

Everything you might want to check before you visit or place a celebration order.

Do you make custom and photo cakes?
Yes. Design and photo cakes need 24 hours' notice. Call +91 63608 14616 with the flavour, weight and design and we will confirm the price before booking.
What are your opening hours?
We open at 8:30 AM and close at 11:30 PM, all seven days. Bread comes out of the oven early morning, so come before noon for the freshest stock.
Can I order on WhatsApp?
Yes. Send your order on +91 63608 14616 and we will confirm availability, price and pickup time. UPI and cards are accepted at the counter.
How long do your cakes stay fresh?
Cream cakes are best within 24 hours if refrigerated. Dry bakes like cookies, rusks and tea cakes stay fresh for a week in an airtight box.
Are eggless cakes available?
Most of our cakes, pastries and cookies are available eggless at the same price. Tell us while ordering and we will make it fresh for you.
