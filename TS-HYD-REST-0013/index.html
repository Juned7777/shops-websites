Our story

A kitchen that has not changed its masala.

It began as a small kitchen in Banjara Hills serving home-style curries and tandoor rotis. Word travelled the old-fashioned way — one satisfied family at a time.

Today the room is bigger and the menu longer, but the rules are unchanged: fresh masala, cooking to order, and no reheated gravies. Everything from the paneer to the biryani stock is prepared in our kitchen.

Questions

Things guests ask us

Everything you might want to check before you come for lunch or book a party.

Do you deliver or offer parcel?
Parcel is available all day and we deliver nearby on our own for larger orders. For bulk party orders, a day's notice on +91 80055 98005 helps us plan.
Is alcohol served?
No, we are a family restaurant and do not serve alcohol. Mocktails, chaas, fresh lime and soft drinks are available.
Do you take table bookings?
Tables for two to four are first-come, first-served. For groups of six or more — birthdays, family functions or office lunches — call +91 80055 98005 and we will keep a table ready.
Do you charge a service charge?
No. Prices are inclusive of taxes and there is no service charge added to the bill.
Is there parking?
Street parking is available outside and there is a paid lot within walking distance. Evenings after 7:30 PM are the busiest.
