Our story

A bakery that starts at 4 AM.

We began with one oven, a second-hand mixer and a rule that nothing sits overnight. The first tray of bread went out at dawn and the neighbourhood noticed by the second week.

Every recipe is tested in our own kitchen before it reaches the counter. If a bake is not good enough for our own family, it does not go on display.

Questions

Good to know

Everything you might want to check before you visit or place a celebration order.

Do you make custom and photo cakes?
Yes. Design and photo cakes need 24 hours' notice. Call +91 93742 46573 with the flavour, weight and design and we will confirm the price before booking.
Do you deliver?
We deliver nearby on our own for larger orders. Smaller orders are pickup from the shop, packed ready for you.
Are eggless cakes available?
Most of our cakes, pastries and cookies are available eggless at the same price. Tell us while ordering and we will make it fresh for you.
Is there parking?
Two-wheelers can park right outside and there is street parking for cars nearby. Morning hours are the busiest.
Can I order on WhatsApp?
Yes. Send your order on +91 93742 46573 and we will confirm availability, price and pickup time. UPI and cards are accepted at the counter.
